Profile

Hello! I am currently a Junior Research Fellow (PhD Scholar) at the Department of Physics, Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi, working under the supervision of Prof. Vaibhav Pant. Driven by a broad interest in astrophysics, my current work focuses primarily on solar physics.

Prior to joining IIT Delhi, I completed my Masters in Physics at IIT Guwahati. With my strong background in programming, mathematical physics, and theoretical physics, my doctoral research focuses on the dynamics of the solar atmosphere, space weather, and magnetohydrodynamics. I am ready to devote myself completely to this purpose.

Research Interests & Projects

I have recently begun my doctoral research focusing on solar magnetohydrodynamics (MHD). My previous academic projects involve utilizing mathematical and theoretical physics alongside computational methods to analyze complex astrophysical systems.

Magnetohydrodynamics in Accretion Flows

Astrophysics Black Holes

Investigated the global structure of advection-dominated accretion flows around Schwarzschild black holes using a magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) framework. Computed global transonic solutions to analyze the formation of centrifugally supported shock waves, mass-loss effects, and post-shock corona properties under the influence of radiative cooling and toroidal magnetic fields.

Supervisor: Prof. Santabrata Das, IIT Guwahati

Report PDF

Path Integral Formulation of Quantum Mechanics

Quantum Mechanics Theoretical Physics

Analyzed quantum systems using path integral and operator approaches to link kernels to physical observables and calculate many-body partition functions. This work explored foundational concepts bridging classical trajectories with quantum probability amplitudes.

Supervisor: Prof. Justin R. David, IISc Bangalore
